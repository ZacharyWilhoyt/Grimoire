Subtitle renderer

WebAssembly assets). Wrapper copyright/license: /licenses/jassub.txt.

Upstream source and build instructions, including submodule source dependencies:
https://github.com/ThaUnknown/jassub
https://www.npmjs.com/package/jassub/v/2.4.1
